Satisfactory Analyzer

A Satisfactory save is a compressed pile of a few thousand objects. Drop one in and it opens into the factory: where you are in the tech tree, every building and what it is making, the belts and the power, everything in every box, and, given a folder of saves, the order in which it all happened.

One save, read in full.

Your saves live in %LOCALAPPDATA%\FactoryGame\Saved\SaveGames\<id> on Windows, and in the server's saved/server folder on a dedicated server. It only ever reads the file.
